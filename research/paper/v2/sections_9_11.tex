\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Analysis and Subsystem Boundaries}

\reharness{} models common GPIO, IRQ, platform, PCI, AMBA, virtio, clock,
power-management, SPI, USB, file-operation, regmap, I2C/SMBus, and MFD paths.
Network, DMA-engine, display, and regulator lifecycles still require models.
Recursion, backward \texttt{goto}s, unbounded loops, and general abstract-store
fixpoints remain readiness blockers; optional SVF is outside the default
configuration.

Computed register-bank addresses are retained as runtime expressions and lowered when every term is bound. Expressions containing unknown calls, unbound members, or \texttt{Top} remain blockers rather than being assigned fabricated symbolic names. Across the evaluation, \EvalComputed{} of \EvalOps{} operations use this computed-address representation.

\subsection{What the Verification Gate Establishes}

Compilation is separate from strict artifact readiness. Under the deterministic
baseline, harness and bare-metal outputs compile for \HarnessCompileCount{}/\EvalDrivers{}
drivers and Linux for \LinuxCompileCount{}/\EvalDrivers{}; only
\LinuxReadyCount{}/\EvalDrivers{} Linux outputs satisfy modeled readiness.
Unknown transforms, unsafe addresses, unbound state, and incomplete lifecycles
are blockers, not source-level equivalence claims.

LLM output may violate prompts or vary across models and samples. Compilation,
receipt accounting, primitive-shape checks, and exercised traces constrain the
reported scope only; they do not reject every extra side effect or prove guard
equivalence and arbitrary hardware equivalence. The trusted base is the pinned
toolchain, extractor, frozen JSON, receipt producer, and oracle; malicious
devices, DMA corruption, interrupt races, and lifecycle failures are out of scope.

Functional-state operations cover selected cursors, counters, and output stores,
but the general MMIO oracle does not observe them; only the DesignWare artifact
checks inspect this data flow. Interrupt-spanning state machines, DMA
lifecycles, concurrency, and physical SPI validation remain open.

The exactly-once receipt mechanism has one structural boundary, surfaced by
the mutation study (Section~\ref{sec:dw-apb-ssi}): seventeen DesignWare
receipts annotate register operations carried by loops whose trip count the
extractor marks unbounded, so the strict lowering plan cannot authorize an
exactly-once cardinality claim for them while receipt accounting still
demands a receipt per contract operation. No artifact satisfies both
constraints; the flagship artifact therefore passes compilation, receipt
accounting, and the primitive-shape check, and is accepted through the
case-specific 18-item checklist rather than the corpus-wide strict gate.
A design path to remove the tension exists but is not implemented: the
harness register-file log already records every MMIO access with its anchor
provenance, so the runtime trace oracle could count receipts dynamically---a
per-iteration multiplicity (receipt observed at least once per loop
iteration, or an interval bound derived from the loop summary) would
discharge exactly-once on unbounded loops without weakening the
anchor-to-primitive binding. Until such loop-aware receipts are
implemented, data-moving drivers whose core is a FIFO loop are outside the
general strict gate; we report this as a measured limitation rather than
routing them through it.

\subsection{Scalability and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

Parsing kernel translation units dominates runtime (measured per-driver
extraction takes \EvalSecondsMin--\EvalSecondsMax{} seconds, median
\EvalSecondsMedian{}, on the evaluation machine; the multi-source
pipelines take 28.5--\MultiSourceDwcTwoSeconds{} seconds each). The
multi-source evaluation covers \MultiSourceTUs{} units and
\MultiSourceLines{} lines; whole-kernel analysis would need persistent caches
and parallel execution.

Internal validity depends on libclang, macro resolution, and IR merging; the
\texttt{(function, offset)} key can collide for repeated same-address accesses.
QEMU covers two deterministic protocol slices. The corpus lacks network
drivers and physical-hardware validation, and the LLM records remain
single-trial, single-endpoint samples without repeated sampling (model,
temperature, and endpoint are recorded; Section~\ref{sec:dw-apb-ssi}). RIS quality, synthesis eligibility, and readiness
are internal metrics, not precision/recall. Required follow-up evidence is
listed in the experiment-debt ledger versioned with the artifact.

A frozen zero-shot holdout (12 drivers excluded from all implementation
work, enforced by an in-tree generalization guard) is versioned with the
artifact and currently reports \ZeroShotCompile{}/\ZeroShotCases{} drivers
compiling on all three deterministic backends, strict readiness of
\ZeroShotHarnessStrict{}/\ZeroShotCases{} (harness and bare-metal) and
\ZeroShotLinuxStrict{}/\ZeroShotCases{} (Linux), with \ZeroShotAllStrict{}
cases strict on all backends and \ZeroShotHardwareCases{} cases containing
modeled hardware interactions; these counts are now reported with the
main evaluation (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}), and the composition
analysis below explains the rate difference.

The holdout's higher strict-readiness rate than the development corpus
(\ZeroShotHarnessStrict{}/\ZeroShotCases{} versus
\HarnessReadyCount{}/\EvalDrivers{}) is a composition effect rather than a
leakage signal. Strict failures in both sets concentrate in drivers whose
subsystem models are absent or incomplete: the development corpus contains
five AHCI storage drivers and three clock drivers that are all blocked
(0/8 strict), while the holdout samples no AHCI driver and its three clock
and one virtio cases fail with the same call-context and effective-plan
blockers (0/4). Within the GPIO and SDHCI classes that both sets sample, the
residual difference tracks driver complexity: the development corpus's
failing SDHCI cases are the far-difficulty ColdFire and AT91 variants, and
its failing GPIO cases carry unsafe computed register addresses
(\emph{mb86s7x}) or inlined-helper definitions without proven call context
(\emph{sodaville}, \emph{cadence}).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \reharness{}, a constrained hybrid translator that extracts
recognized device-core evidence from complete Linux drivers. AST--IR analysis
recovers selected register, transaction, and state operations; an LLM emits
four backend forms, which are accepted only within the stated compile, receipt,
shape, and exercised-trace checks.

Across \EvalDrivers{} drivers and \MultiSourceDrivers{} multi-source modules,
the results support an evidence-boundary prototype, not whole-driver equivalence;
stronger claims remain contingent on the deferred experiments recorded with the
artifact.
