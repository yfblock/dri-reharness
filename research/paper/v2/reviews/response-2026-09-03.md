Point-by-point response to the 2026-09-03 re-review. Each item lists the
change made (file/section) or the honest limitation accepted. Numbers cite
the regenerated `generated_results.tex` macros and versioned result JSONs.

## W1 — single-driver, single-trial LLM record

- Added a repeated-trial measurement (`qa/verification/repeated_trials.py`,
  results in `research/experiments/results/dw-repeated-trials.json`): k=5
  independent trials of the constrained pipeline on the pinned DesignWare
  extraction, per backend (harness, bare-metal, Linux), measuring the SAME
  candidate at three stages — raw first pass (compile-repair disabled),
  post compile-repair (pipeline budget), post receipt-repair (the
  bounded repair_lowering protocol, guards included) — with the gate's own
  check states recorded per stage. The Rust backend remains single-trial
  (the pipeline's generation fan-out covers the three C backends); this is
  stated in the paper. Numbers in §7.6.
- The remaining gap is acknowledged: trials remain on one driver family
  and one (private) endpoint; repeated trials across the five
  synthesis-eligible drivers are still listed in the experiment-debt ledger.

## W2 — pristine flagship rejected by the strict gate; loop receipts

- Unchanged in substance (the structural 17-loop-carried-receipt conflict
  is disclosed in §6.6 and §7). New: §7 now sketches the concrete design
  path the reviewer asks about — per-iteration dynamic receipt counting in
  the runtime trace oracle (the harness register-file log already records
  every MMIO access with anchor provenance; an interval-bounded
  multiplicity derived from the loop summary would discharge exactly-once
  on unbounded loops without weakening the anchor-to-primitive binding).
  Marked explicitly as not implemented; until then FIFO-loop data movers
  are accepted at the checklist-backed level, reported as a measured
  limitation.

## W3 — baseline confounded with repair budget; no C2Rust comparison

- The direct-LLM baseline tool gained `--compile-repair-rounds`, giving the
  baseline candidate the same compile-diagnostic repair budget the
  constrained pipeline grants its own candidates (probe-compile, feed back
  exact `cc` diagnostics, bounded rounds, whole-file rewrite). The
  re-run's pre- and post-repair gate verdicts are both recorded in
  `research/experiments/results/direct-llm-baseline.json` and reported in
  §7.6.
- Added the existing-tool comparison the first review asked for
  (`qa/verification/existing_tool_comparison.py`, table in §6, inputs
  versioned under `research/experiments/existing-tools/`): on the QEMU
  `edu` device, QEMU's own hand-written model (v9.0.0 `hw/misc/edu.c`),
  C2Rust 0.22.1, and the reharness backends. C2Rust cannot transpile the
  real driver (kernel headers crash it in three stages); its transpilation
  of a de-kernelized stub with the same register protocol is 39 lines with
  the protocol implicit in pointer arithmetic and 100% unsafe function
  signatures, with no contract to reconcile against. QEMU's model serves
  11 MMIO offsets (the device view); the driver touches 3; the reharness
  RIS covers those 3/3 with per-operation receipts.

   implementation remains future work.

   ledger.
