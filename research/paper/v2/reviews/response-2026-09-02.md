## W1 / Q1 — LLM case fails its own compilation gate; single driver

- All four DesignWare backends were regenerated through the LLM bridge
  (`reharness gen`, model and sampling parameters recorded in
  `research/experiments/results/llm-run-metadata.json`), then run through
  the bounded repair loop (`qa/verification/repair_artifact.py`) with the
  exact per-backend compiler diagnostics. Repair rounds per backend are
  recorded in `research/experiments/results/artifact-repair-log.json`.
  §7.6 and the abstract now report the post-repair checklist state with
  the model/temperature/rounds metadata. Post-repair, all 18 checklist
  items pass on all four backends
  (`research/experiments/results/dw-apb-ssi-checklist.json`).
- Honest provenance note (also in §7.6): the harness backend's installed
  artifact was closed by a deterministic derivation from the repaired
  bare-metal pair (include rename plus an appended oracle `main()`, with
  receipt-count and brace-balance guards) after the endpoint stopped
  serving the long chunked generation; the harness contract differs from
  the bare-metal contract only in the host build environment. The
  derivation is recorded in the repair log as a deterministic run. The
  other three backends are LLM-emitted end to end.
- The closed-loop v2 record (§7.5, "Closed-loop LLM record") is now
  reported: 2 source-build closed-loop experiments, 1 accepted (edu:
  value-oracle pass after 2 repair rounds, 6/6 generated-function runtime
  coverage vs the 5/6 in-tree baseline), 1 rejected (ftgpio010 after 4
  rounds).
- Regenerating the artifacts surfaced an extraction regression that the
  review's gate soundness concern (Q2) effectively anticipated: the
  IR-primary refactor (69d2dff) had dropped the driver-private MMIO wrapper
  layouts (`dw_read_io_reg`/`dw_write_io_reg`/`dw_readl`/`dw_writel`,
  `dwc2_*`) from `src/extractor/mmio.py`, so switch-based accessors lost
  their `offset` argument during inlining and the Data Register surfaced as
  offset `0x0` with the register absent from the inferred register map.
  The layouts are restored and declared in the zero-shot holdout
  specialization allowlist (both v1 and v2 manifests); re-extraction now
  yields all 19 registers including `DW_SPI_DR` at 0x60, symbolic DR leaf
  operations in the RIS, and IR--AST coverage of 100% (previously 99%,
  1 missing op). All DW numbers in the paper are regenerated from this
  fixed extraction.
- The bounded repair tooling was extended in response to observed raw-
  emission failure modes, and each extension is versioned in the repair
  log: (a) receipt-repair batches at most 30 missing operations per round
  so one response cannot exceed the endpoint's output cap; (b) deterministic
  normalization pre-passes (anchor-name canonicalization to the doubled
  labels and transaction anchor names, digest rewriting from
  the contract after transport corruption, register-receipt-to-transaction-
  marker conversion, duplicate-receipt removal, and mechanical merging of
  whole-function re-emissions into their base definitions) repair metadata
  and structure only, with register-access semantics still verified by the
  anchor bodies, compilation, and the ordered data-flow items; (c) every
  repair response must pass guards that reject receipt loss, brace
  imbalance, or an unclosed comment before it can touch the artifact (a
  truncated response had previously spliced into and destroyed an
  artifact---the guards make that class of damage impossible); (d) a third
  bounded loop completes the buffer-side transfer-loop dataflow (tx/rx
  cursor advance, length decrement, FIFO-to-buffer store) that receipts do
  not cover, guided by the upstream-shaped reference fragment and checked
  by the same checklist patterns.
- Not done: repeated trials across the 5 synthesis-eligible drivers
  (single-trial records remain; stated in §7.6).

## W4 / Q2 — no mutation study of the gate

- Added `qa/verification/gate_mutation_study.py`: five injected-fault
  mutants of the generated harness artifact — invented MMIO write
  (extra_write), dropped interrupt-ack read (drop_irq_ack), narrowed
  access width (width_shrink), early buffer-cursor advance
  (reorder_rx_cursor), and well-formed receipt with the hardware access
  deleted (receipt_without_semantics) — each run through the unchanged
  verification pipeline (the pipeline's own repair loop disabled during
  replay so the study measures the gate, not the repair). All five
  mutants are rejected: extra_write and width_shrink by the AST-leaf
  primitive-shape check (unanchored primitive; width mismatch),
  drop_irq_ack by receipt accounting, receipt_without_semantics by the
  primitive-shape check (empty anchor body vs expected shape — receipts
  are bound to the primitive calls they annotate), and reorder_rx_cursor
  at the strict lowering-plan stage. The pristine artifact is itself
  rejected by the strict lowering plan, and the study reports that
  baseline honestly: 17 receipts annotate register ops carried by loops
  the extractor marks unbounded, and the plan's conservative policy
  refuses to authorize receipts inside such loops while receipt
  accounting still demands one per contract op — the artifact keeps the
  accesses (required for runnable transfer behavior) and passes
  compilation, receipt accounting, and the AST-leaf check. The
  early-cursor mutant shares the pristine's stage: buffer-cursor order is
  invisible to the op-level checks inside the gate; the fault is exposed
  by the checklist's ordered-dataflow items, which sit outside the gate
  (stated as a limitation in §7.6). Getting the pristine replay this far
  also required fixing a real normalization bug: the repair tool had been
  collapsing the generator's canonical doubled anchor labels
  (`__rh_op_op_24` → `__rh_op_24`), which made the AST-leaf oracle
  report 150 missing anchors on every backend; the installed artifacts
  were re-canonicalized (anchor doubling, `__rh_txn_` transaction
  anchors, anchor-body primitive spellings, non-primitive buffer-side
  dataflow helpers) under receipt-count and brace-balance guards, and the
  repair tool now emits the canonical forms. Results with the first
  failing check per mutant are reported in §7.6 and versioned in
  `research/experiments/results/dw-gate-mutation-study.json`.
