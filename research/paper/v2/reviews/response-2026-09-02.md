## W1 / Q1 — LLM case fails its own compilation gate; single driver

- All four DesignWare backends were regenerated through the LLM bridge
  (`reharness gen`, model and sampling parameters recorded in
  `research/experiments/results/llm-run-metadata.json`), then run through
  the bounded repair loop (`qa/verification/repair_artifact.py`) with the
  exact per-backend compiler diagnostics. Repair rounds per backend are
  recorded in `research/experiments/results/artifact-repair-log.json`.
  §7.6 and the abstract now report the post-repair checklist state with
  the model/temperature/rounds metadata.
- The closed-loop v2 record (§7.5, "Closed-loop LLM record") is now
  reported: 2 source-build closed-loop experiments, 1 accepted (edu:
  value-oracle pass after 2 repair rounds, 6/6 generated-function runtime
  coverage vs the 5/6 in-tree baseline), 1 rejected (ftgpio010 after 4
  rounds).
- Regenerating the artifacts surfaced an extraction regression that the
  review's gate soundness concern (Q2) effectively anticipated: the
  IR-primary refactor (69d2dff) had dropped the driver-private MMIO wrapper
  layouts (`dw_read_io_reg`/`dw_write_io_reg`/`dw_readl`/`dw_writel`,
  `dwc2_*`) from `src/extractor/mmio.py`, so switch-based accessors lost
  their `offset` argument during inlining and the Data Register surfaced as
  offset `0x0` with the register absent from the inferred register map.
  The layouts are restored and declared in the zero-shot holdout
  specialization allowlist (both v1 and v2 manifests); re-extraction now
  yields all 19 registers including `DW_SPI_DR` at 0x60, symbolic DR leaf
  operations in the RIS, and IR--AST coverage of 100% (previously 99%,
  1 missing op). All DW numbers in the paper are regenerated from this
  fixed extraction.
- Not done: repeated trials across the 5 synthesis-eligible drivers
  (single-trial records remain; stated in §7.6).
